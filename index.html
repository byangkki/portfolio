📜 How to Add Free MIT Licenses

Adding a license shields you from legal liability and proves authorship. It costs nothing and takes 30 seconds!

Step 1
Click "Add Free MIT License"
Click the purple license button above for any project you want to protect.
Step 2
Choose Template
GitHub will ask to choose a license. Click "Choose a license template" on the right.
Step 3
Select MIT License
Select MIT License from the left menu and click "Review and submit".
Step 4
Commit File
Click the green "Commit changes..." button. Your repository is now legally protected!
